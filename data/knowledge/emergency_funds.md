# Emergency Funds & Savings Buffer

An emergency fund is a pool of cash set aside to cover unexpected life events—such as medical emergencies, job loss, auto repairs, or urgent home maintenance. Having a dedicated reserve prevents you from relying on high-interest credit cards or loans during crisis periods.

## Why Emergency Funds Are Critical for Debt Recovery
Without an emergency fund, your debt recovery plan is highly vulnerable. Any unexpected cost forces you to borrow more, disrupting your repayment momentum. A savings buffer breaks this cycle by providing a liquid cash cushion.

## How Much to Save
- **Starter Fund**: Before aggressively paying off debt with extra payments, build a starter emergency fund of **$1,000 or ₹50,000**. This covers basic minor emergencies.
- **Full Reserve**: Once high-interest debts are cleared, expand the fund to cover **3 to 6 months of essential living expenses**. If your employment is variable (e.g. freelance or self-employed), target 6 to 9 months of expenses.
- **Where to Store**: Keep the fund in a separate, liquid account (like a high-yield savings account) so it is accessible but not tempted for daily spending.
