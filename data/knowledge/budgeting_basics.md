# Personal Finance Basics & Budgeting 101

Budgeting is the foundational pillar of personal finance and debt recovery. It is a systematic process of tracking income, identifying expense habits, and allocating disposable cash flow towards financial targets like saving or paying off debt.

## The 50/30/20 Budgeting Rule
One popular budgeting framework is the 50/30/20 rule, which suggests dividing your monthly net income into three distinct spending categories:
- **50% for Essentials (Needs)**: Rent, utilities, groceries, healthcare, transportation, and minimum debt payments. These are critical costs required to sustain your daily life.
- **30% for Discretionary Spending (Wants)**: Dining out, subscriptions, hobbies, shopping, travel, and entertainment.
- **20% for Savings & Debt Paydown**: Extra payments on debts above the minimum, emergency fund contributions, and retirement investments.

## Essential vs. Discretionary Expenses
Distinguishing between essential and discretionary expenses is vital for creating extra cash surplus to accelerate debt recovery. 
- **Essential expenses** cannot be easily avoided or reduced in the short term.
- **Discretionary expenses** represent lifestyle choices that can be suspended or eliminated in a financial crunch. Trim wants first when launching a debt recovery plan.
