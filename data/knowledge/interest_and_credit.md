# Interest Rates, Compounding & Credit Utilization

Understanding how lenders price money and calculate balances is key to mastering debt relief and credit recovery.

## Interest Rates & Annual Percentage Rate (APR)
- **Interest Rate**: The percentage of principal charged by the lender for borrowing money.
- **APR (Annual Percentage Rate)**: The true cost of borrowing, which includes the interest rate plus any loan origination fees, service charges, or mandatory insurance. Always compare loans using APR rather than basic interest rates.

## The Danger of Compounding Interest in Debt
For credit cards, interest compounds daily or monthly. This means you pay interest on your interest.
- If you make only the **minimum monthly payment** on a credit card, the payment barely covers the interest added. The principal balance remains almost unchanged, trapping you in a debt cycle that can last decades.

## Credit Utilization Ratio
The Credit Utilization Ratio represents how much of your total available credit you are using:
`Credit Utilization = (Total Outstanding Credit Card Balance / Total Credit Limit) * 100`
- Keep this ratio **under 30%** to avoid damaging your credit score. Lowering utilization is one of the fastest ways to improve credit profiles.
